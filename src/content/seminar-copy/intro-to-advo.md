# Intro to AdvO: page copy

This file is the marketing text on the Intro to AdvO page. Change any words you
like and the page changes with it. Three rules keep it working:

1. Keep every line that starts with "##" or "###" (the sections and questions).
2. Keep the labels at the start of a line: Label, Subtitle, Button, Heading,
   Intro, Closing, Photo, Note, Good fit, Not the best fit. Change only the words after the colon.
3. A line starting with "- " is one item in a list. Where an item has a title,
   write it as "- Title: the text". Add or remove items freely.

Other text in a section is ordinary paragraphs; leave a blank line between
paragraphs. To drop a whole section from the page, delete it, heading and all.
Dates, prices, the agenda and the instructors are set elsewhere and are not in
this file.

## Questions

Yes. The course is free for everyone, with no membership required.

### How long is it?
About two hours, self-paced. Watch it all at once or a module at a time.

### Do I need any experience?
No. There are no prerequisites.

### Can students take it?
Yes. Chiropractic students are welcome.

### What comes after the Intro?
The Fundamentals Series, taught live on Zoom in the Monthly Huddle for AOI members, and hands-on training at an AdvO Intensive or Bootcamp.
